# Asclepios AI -- Final Presentation

## Project Overview

**Asclepios AI** is a data-driven treatment optimization platform
designed for **Substance Use Disorder (SUD)**. Our solution leverages
machine learning to predict optimal treatment duration, reduce
recidivism, and improve resource allocation in rehabilitation
facilities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem Statement

Current SUD treatment faces significant challenges:

- **30% readmission rate** within one year\
- **High recidivism** due to premature discharge\
- **Lack of standardization** in treatment personalization\
- **Resource mismatches** between patient needs and facility capacity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Research Question

> *"How can we predict optimal treatment duration to minimize relapse
> risk?"*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Solution: Asclepios AI Smart Recommendation

Our platform provides:

- **Predict Duration** -- Calculates optimal length of stay per
    patient profile\
- **Reduce Readmissions** -- Ensures treatment is not cut short\
- **Optimize Resources** -- Smart allocation of beds, staff, and
    clinical time\
- **Personalize Care** -- Tailored recovery plans based on individual
    data

------------------------------------------------------------------------
